This update brings bug fixes and is recommended to all users. The Toon version is now available in the VCC Repository, GitHub, and on BOOTH.

## Changes & Fixes
- Fixed white PBR map channels when locking materials.
- Fixed Lil Fur masks not being preserved when upgrading from 9.3 to 10.0.
- Fixed DirectX CBuffers overflowing in instanced lighting.
- Fixed Receive Shadows setting in Skin Shadows mode.
- Fixed Lil Fur emission in additional light passes.

## ThryEditor 2.74.1
- Fixed Cubemap filter picking.
- Fixed Cubemap validation in texture fields.
