This update contains updated a magnitude of lighting fixes and optimizations, among other fixes. The Toon version is now available in the VCC Repository, GitHub, and on BOOTH.

### Changes & Fixes
- Updated VRC Light Volumes 3.0, including a magnitude of lighting improvements.
- Fixed NaN issues in Matcap Light Direction mode.
- Upgrading materials that have leftover 7.3 props now keep 9.x lighting instead.
- Removed Duplicate Presets, fixing some unwanted Warning messages.
