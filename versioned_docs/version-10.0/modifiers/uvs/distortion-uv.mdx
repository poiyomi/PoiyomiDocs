Distortion UV refers to a shader technique where the UV coordinates used to sample a texture are offset or warped to create the illusion of distortion or other wobbly effects.

This section houses the global settings for Distortion UV. To use it, select it as a UV option in supported sections of the shader.

## Distortion UV

<Property type="toggle" />

Enables Distortion UV effects on the material. Make sure to select which Texture Slots across the shader should use Distortion UV.

## Distorted UV

<Property type="dropdown" options={['UV0', 'UV1', 'UV2', 'UV3', 'Panosphere', 'World Pos XZ', 'Local Pos', 'Polar UV']} />

Sets which UV set to use for the distortion effect.

## Mask

<Property type="texture" texture="data" />

Masks where the Distortion effect should only appear in.

## Distortion Mode

<Property type="dropdown" options={['Texture', 'Gerstner Waves', 'Simplex Noise', 'Voronoi', 'Curl Noise', 'Fractal Brownian Motion']} default="Texture" />

Switches the Distortion method to use on the material.

## Tiling

<Property type="vector2">
[Distortion Mode](#distortion-mode) is set to `Gerstner Waves`, `Simplex Noise`, `Voronoi`, `Curl Noise`, or `Fractal Brownian Motion`
</Property>

Sets the tiling of the custom Distortion on all modes EXCEPT `Texture`.

## Pan Speed

<Property type="vector2">
[Distortion Mode](#distortion-mode) is set to `Gerstner Waves`, `Simplex Noise`, `Voronoi`, `Curl Noise`, or `Fractal Brownian Motion`
</Property>

Sets the panning speed of the custom Distortion on all modes EXCEPT `Texture`.

## Texture Mode

This area is shown when [Distortion Mode](#distortion-mode) is configured to use `Texture`.

### Distortion Texture 1

<Property type="texture" texture="data" />

Linear texture of the first distortion.

### Distortion Texture 2

<Property type="texture" texture="data" />

Linear texture of the second distortion.

### Strength 1

<Property type="float" />

Sets the strength of the Distortion from Texture 1.

### Strength 2

<Property type="float" />

Sets the strength of the Distortion from Texture 2.

## Gerstner Waves

This area is shown when [Distortion Mode](#distortion-mode) is configured to use `Gerstner Waves`.

Gerstner Waves is an effect known in fluid dynamics for periodic surface gravitational waves. [More Info](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trochoidal_wave).

### Wave Speed

<Property type="range" min="0.1" max="5" />

Total speed of the waves.

### Wave Density

<Property type="range" min="0.01" max="100" />

Density of the waves.

### Flow Strength

<Property type="range" min="0" max="1" />

Total strength of the entire wave flow.

### Wave

:::info[Documentation Info]
Each of the settings below appears for Waves 1, 2, 3, and 4. They are independent of each other.
:::

#### Direction

<Property type="range" min="0" max="360" />

Direction of the Wave in a rotational angle.

#### Amplitude

<Property type="range" min="0" max="1" />

The amplitude intensity of the Wave.

#### Wavelength

<Property type="range" min="0.5" max="20" />

Total length of the Wave.

#### Speed Mult

<Property type="range" min="0" max="10" />

Multiplier value of the Wave speed.

## Simplex Noise

This area is shown when [Distortion Mode](#distortion-mode) is configured to use `Simplex Noise`.

This is a type of gradient coherent noise used to generate smooth, natural-looking random patterns. [More Info](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simplex_noise)

### Scale

<Property type="range" min="0.01" max="50" />

Sets the total scale of the noise.

### Strength

<Property type="range" min="0" max="1" />

Sets the total strength of the noise effect.

### Speed

<Property type="range" min="0" max="5" />

Sets how fast the noise is generated.

## Voronoi

This area is shown when [Distortion Mode](#distortion-mode) is configured to use `Voronoi`.

Similar to [Voronoi](/special-fx/voronoi.mdx), it is an effect that generates a noise pattern in 2D or 3D, making for very interesting stylized effects.

### Scale

<Property type="range" min="0.01" max="50" />

Sets the total scale of the Voronoi.

### Strength

<Property type="range" min="0" max="1" />

Sets the total strength of the Voronoi.

### Speed

<Property type="range" min="0" max="5" />

Sets the total speed of the Voronoi.

### Edge Bleed

<Property type="range" min="0" max="1" />

Sets how much to bleed the edge of the Voronoi.

## Curl Noise

This area is shown when [Distortion Mode](#distortion-mode) is configured to use `Curl Noise`.

Curl Noise is a generated noise that turns into swirling motion through the application of a curl operator in the shader's code. This results in interesting fluid-like patterns without having to run a full fluid simulation. [Example Reference](https://al-ro.github.io/projects/curl/)

### Scale

<Property type="range" min="0.01" max="1" />

Sets the total scale of the Curl Noise.

### Strength

<Property type="range" min="0" max="0.5" />

Sets the total strength of the Curl Noise.

### Speed

<Property type="range" min="0" max="5" />

Sets the total speed of how fast the Curl Noise is generated in the loop.

## Fractal Brownian Motion

This area is shown when [Distortion Mode](#distortion-mode) is configured to use `Fractal Brownian Motion`.

In the theory of probability, a [fractional Brownian motion (fBm)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fractional_Brownian_motion) is the generalization of [Brownian motion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brownian_motion).

### Scale

<Property type="range" min="0.01" max="2" />

Sets the total scale of the motion.

### Strength

<Property type="range" min="0" max="1" />

Sets the total strength of the motion.

### Speed

<Property type="range" min="0" max="5" />

Sets the total speed of the motion

### Octaves

<Property type="range" min="1" max="8" />

Sets the total amount of octaves to apply to the motion.

### Lacunarity

<Property type="range" min="1" max="4" />

Sets the total amount of lacunarity to apply to the motion.

### Gain

<Property type="range" min="0" max="1" />

How much gain to apply to the motion.

## Audio Link

:::info
The settings in this section will only be visible when [Audio Link](/audio-link/audio-link.mdx) is activated on the Material.
:::

### Strength 1 Band

<Property type="dropdown" options={['Bass', 'Low Mid', 'High Mid', 'Treble', 'Volume']} />

Which Band to use for adjusting Strength 1 when Audio Link is present.

### Strength 1 Offset Range

<Property type="float2" />

How much to add or subtract from Strength 1 in reaction to the selected Audio Link Band.

| Modifier | Function |
| --- | --- |
| Min | Amount changed to Strength 1 with No Audio in Strength 1 Band |
| Max | Amount changed to Strength 1 with Max Audio in Strength 1 Band |

### Strength 2 Band

<Property type="dropdown" options={['Bass', 'Low Mid', 'High Mid', 'Treble', 'Volume']} />

Which Band to use for adjusting Strength 2 when Audio Link is present.

### Strength 2 Offset Range

<Property type="float2" />

How much to add or subtract from Strength 2 in reaction to the selected Audio Link Band.

| Modifier | Function |
| --- | --- |
| Min | Amount changed to Strength 2 with No Audio in Strength 2 Band |
| Max | Amount changed to Strength 2 with Max Audio in Strength 2 Band |
